# The brief — 11.6, the capstone: a franchise

Teacher-only, and **deliberately not in the student folder** — same reason as the others.

## What this brief has to do, which is different from the other two

The capstone hands the learner **inherited artifacts**: a bloated specification, an incomplete test
suite, and a partial implementation, all produced by a weak agent and then chosen for their failure
profile. Their job is to fix what needs fixing and rebuild the implementation.

That only works if **this brief is the authority.** Every claim in the inherited specification must be
decidable against it: either the brief settles the matter and the claim is right or wrong, or the brief
leaves it open and the claim is a legitimate choice the learner may keep — but must then own and state.

So the brief is short on purpose, and every sentence in it is load-bearing.

## What it settles — use these to judge an inherited claim wrong

- a chain has several shops
- each shop already has its own admission rule and its own promotions
- the chain has an admission rule and promotions of its own as well
- the answer names **where** to buy and **for how much**
- **cheapest**, not first, not nearest, not any that will take it
- a duck nobody will sell has no answer at all
- whole units

## What it leaves open — these are choices, not errors

1. **Chain rule against shop rule.** Must both admit? Is the chain a veto only? A default for shops
   that have no rule of their own? All three are real franchise models.
2. **Whose promotions apply, and in what order.** The chain's, the shop's, both in sequence, or the
   better of the two. If both, order changes the number.
3. **Ties.** Two shops at the same price — the answer names a shop, so **the tie is observable** and a
   specification has to say something.
4. **Cheapest measured before or after the chain's promotions.** If a chain promotion is conditional,
   these can name *different shops*. Subtle, and the place a weak implementation will be naive.
5. **The chain admits and no shop does, or the reverse.**
6. **A chain with no shops.**

## Calibration — NOT DONE YET, and nothing gets built before it is

Two questions, in this order, and the same procedure that saved 11.4 and would have saved 11.5:

1. **Do two readers genuinely diverge?** Especially on ties and on fork 4. If they converge, this
   feature is another seam and the capstone needs a different one. *Do not skip this because the
   feature looks rich — the seam looked rich too.*
2. **Does a weak agent produce the failure profile the capstone needs** — some corner cases passing,
   some not, some ordinary cases passing? That profile has to be *selected* from several runs, not
   hoped for.

Also to check before committing: that a suite of the key corner cases is **passable** by a weak agent,
or the exercise is unwinnable.

---

> We are opening a chain. Each shop keeps the rules it already has — who it will take, and which
> promotions it runs — and the chain has rules of its own on top of that.
>
> A customer with one duck wants to know where in the chain to buy it and what they will pay. Cheapest
> wins. If nobody in the chain will sell them that duck, say so.
>
> Prices are whole units, as before.

The surface to describe:

```kotlin
/** Where to buy [duck] in [franchise] most cheaply, and for how much — or nothing if nobody will. */
fun bestOffer(duck: Duck, franchise: Franchise): Offer?

data class Franchise(
    val name: String,
    val admissionPolicy: AdmissionPolicy,
    val promotions: List<DiscountRule>,
    val shops: List<Shop>,
)

data class Offer(val shop: Shop, val price: Int)

data class Shop(
    val name: String,
    val admissionPolicy: AdmissionPolicy,
    val promotions: List<DiscountRule> = emptyList(),
) {
    fun admits(duck: Duck): Boolean
}
```

`Shop.promotions` is the one addition to `:core` this needs, defaulted so nothing existing changes.

`AdmissionPolicy` and `DiscountRule` are the types from before: four leaves and three combinators for
admission, six rule kinds for promotions. `Duck` is `name`, `price`, `hasKotlinAttribute`, `accessories`.
